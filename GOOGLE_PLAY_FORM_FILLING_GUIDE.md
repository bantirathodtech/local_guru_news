## 🔄 For Future Updates

## 📞 Troubleshooting

If Google Play reviewers report login issues:

1. Verify the test account is active
2. Check that the credentials are correct
3. Test login flow yourself with these exact credentials
4. Ensure no additional verification is required
5. Update the instructions if the login flow has changed
